Our story

It started with a jar of starter and a little too much ambition.

Bramble & Batch is a one-kitchen bakery ran by Abby, right here in Fort Collins. No storefront, no fancy equipment, just a home oven, a Dutch oven that's seen some things, and a sourdough starter that gets fed better than most of us.

Every loaf gets a slow, patient rise. Every cookie gets real brown butter. Everything gets made by hand, in small batches, with a whole lot of heart. Some days that means flour on the ceiling. Worth it.

We're small for now, but the dreams are big. Thanks for being part of the beginning.

Love, Abby
