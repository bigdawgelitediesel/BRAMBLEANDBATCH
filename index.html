The baker

Hi, I'm Abby.

Bramble & Batch started in my home kitchen with a stack of recipes and way too much butter. Once a month I bake a short menu in small batches, set up a table, and sell until the trays are empty.

Everything is made by hand, the week of. No shortcuts, no day-old leftovers. If you see it on the table, it was baked for you.

FAQ

How often do you pop up?

About once a month. The next date is always posted at the top of this page.

Can I pre-order?

Not yet. For now it's first come, first served at the table. Follow on Instagram so you don't miss it.

How do I pay?

Cash and card are both welcome.

Do you do custom orders?

Ask! Send a message on Instagram and we'll see what fits the schedule.

What about allergies?

Everything is baked in a home kitchen that handles wheat, dairy, eggs, and nuts. Ask at the table about any specific item.
